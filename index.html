About Our Initiative

The Pet Adoption and Responsible Pet Care Initiative aims to promote the adoption of stray and rescued animals and create awareness about responsible pet ownership.

Through community engagement, adoption awareness and pet-care guidance, we encourage people to treat animals with care and compassion.
